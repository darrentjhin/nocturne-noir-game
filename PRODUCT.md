# NOCTURNE product position

## Current launch position

NOCTURNE should launch first as a free, controlled browser beta for invited two-player groups. The beta should validate completion, role balance, clarity, session length, and demand for a third file before accounts, payments, matchmaking, or File 03 production.

## Audience promise

- Exactly two players on separate private screens.
- Authored cooperative deduction, not random trivia.
- Different responsibilities for The Street and The Desk.
- A connected noir series with mechanically distinct cases.
- Fast case-code entry for invited pairs during beta.

## Success criteria for wider release

- At least five unfamiliar pairs complete each file without outside coaching.
- At least 80% rate instructions clear or mostly clear.
- At least 70% rate challenge balanced.
- At least 80% rate roles equal or mostly equal.
- At least 70% say the ending felt earned.
- No recurring unrecoverable connection or seat-reservation failure.
- Durable storage, release gating, monitoring, and rollback are active.

## Explicitly deferred

File 03, accounts, payments, matchmaking, built-in voice, AI witnesses, randomized cases, achievements, and multiple server replicas remain out of scope until the beta criteria are met.
