CONNECTION DESK

Help & reconnecting

Play on separate screens

Each detective receives private information. Use separate devices or separate browser profiles. Do not duplicate a tab after joining.

If the signal drops

Refresh or reopen the same browser. Your saved role should reconnect automatically. A private resume link can also move your role to another device; never send that link to your partner.

If a partner cannot return

Their seat remains protected during a short reconnect window. After the countdown, the connected detective can release the abandoned seat and invite a replacement. Releasing clears that detective’s private notes and old resume token.

Pause safely

Copy your private resume link, then close the tab without pressing Release Seat. Active progress remains available for the retention period shown in the lobby.

If a code opens the wrong file

Follow the redirect shown by the game. File 01 and File 02 share room codes but use separate interfaces.

Before reporting a problem

Record the file, detective role, current screen, browser, device, and approximate time. Never include the other detective’s private notes or resume link.
