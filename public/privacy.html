PLAYER INFORMATION

Privacy

NOCTURNE does not require an account. A case temporarily uses the names players enter, a room code, role-specific resume tokens, game progress, Radio messages, and private notebook text so two browsers can play and reconnect.

Private means private from your partner

Your partner cannot read your notebook or resume token. The server may temporarily store that information when durable room storage is enabled. Do not write sensitive personal information in the notebook or Radio Line.

Retention

Inactive rooms expire after up to 24 hours with durable storage, or approximately 30 minutes in memory-only mode. Releasing your seat clears that role’s private notebook and resume token. Restarting clears both notebooks from the prior run.

Feedback

Completion and exit feedback uses structured categories only. It does not include your name, room code, Radio messages, notebook, or free-form comments.

Operational logs

Hosting and security systems may process standard connection information such as IP address, request time, and browser metadata. Game monitoring must not record Radio or notebook contents.
